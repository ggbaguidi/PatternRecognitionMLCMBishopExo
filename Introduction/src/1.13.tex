\begin{tcolorbox}[breakable,colback=gray!5,colframe=gray!60,title=Solution]
The maximum-likelihood variance estimator in (1.56) is
\[
\sigma^2_{ML} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} (x_n - \mu_{ML})^2.
\]

In this exercise, replace the estimate $\mu_{ML}$ by the true mean $\mu$. Define
\[
\widetilde{\sigma}^2 = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} (x_n - \mu)^2.
\]
We calculate its expectation:

\begin{align*}
\mathbb{E}[\widetilde{\sigma}^2]
&= \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} (x_n - \mu)^2\right]
  \quad \text{(definition of $\widetilde{\sigma}^2$)} \\
&= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} \mathbb{E}\left[(x_n - \mu)^2\right]
  \quad \text{(linearity of expectation)} \\
&= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} \operatorname{Var}(x_n)
  \quad \text{(definition of variance around the true mean)} \\
&= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^{N} \sigma^2
  \quad \text{(each $x_n$ has variance $\sigma^2$)} \\
&= \sigma^2
  \quad \text{(there are $N$ identical terms).}
\end{align*}

Therefore,
\[
\boxed{\mathbb{E}[\widetilde{\sigma}^2] = \sigma^2}.
\]
Thus, replacing $\mu_{ML}$ by the true mean $\mu$ removes the downward bias of the maximum-likelihood variance estimator.
\end{tcolorbox}
